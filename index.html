Run Conclusion

Why Algorithm Choice Matters

  • Brute force is always correct but becomes impossible very quickly. For example: 5 stops = 120 routes, 8 stops = 40,320 routes, 15 stops > 1.3 trillion routes.
  • Greedy is very fast and scales to hundreds of stops, but can give a longer route because it only looks one step ahead (local optima).
  • Recommendation: For a small number of stops (≤ 8), use an exact method. For many stops, use Greedy because a slightly longer route is acceptable when exact methods are computationally unfeasible.

Limitations: Distances in this lab are straight-line (Euclidean) without considering traffic, road networks, or multiple vehicles.
